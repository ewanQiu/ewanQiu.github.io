Privacy Policy

Drama Analytics is a private, self-hosted tool used by its owner to view statistics of YouTube channels they own.

It accesses YouTube data only through the official YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API, using read-only permissions granted by the account owner.

All data is stored locally on the owner's own machine. No data is shared with, sold to, or transmitted to any third party.

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
